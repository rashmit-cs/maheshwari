Frequently Asked Questions

Everything people usually ask before booking.

Fares vary by distance, vehicle class and trip type. One-way airport transfers to North Goa are indicated at around ₹1,200–1,900 depending on vehicle. Local sightseeing packages are indicated from ₹1,400 for a 4-hour package up to ₹3,400 for a 12-hour package. Outstation trips are billed per km, indicated from ₹16/km for a 4-seater hatchback. These are example figures: we confirm the fare before you book, so call or WhatsApp for an exact number.
