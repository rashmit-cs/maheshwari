    How the estimate works

    1. Choose the vehicle by seats: 4-seater hatchback, 5-seater sedan, Ertiga-class 6-7 seater, or Innova Crysta / Hycross.
    2. Drag the slider to your distance (5 to 300 km). A round trip doubles it.
    3. Estimated fare = distance × the vehicle's per-km rate. Driver allowance is included and there is no minimum-kilometre charge.
    4. Tolls, interstate permit tax and night-halt charges are billed at actuals.
    5. Tempo Traveller and buses (9 to 40 seats): see the fare table below.
    Price may change. Weekends, public holidays, festival dates, peak season, route changes and waiting time can change the final price. We confirm it before you travel.

    Want fuel prices and a taxi vs self-drive comparison? See the Goa Taxi Fare Calculator. Airport and station fares are on Routes & Fares and Airport Transfers.
