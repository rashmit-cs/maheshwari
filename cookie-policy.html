Cookie Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

The short version

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising pixels or social-media trackers. It uses one small item of session storage only to remember that you closed our WhatsApp enquiry pop-up, so we do not show it again on every page. It is never sent to us or anyone else and is cleared when you close the tab. Because we use no non-essential cookies, we do not show a cookie banner.

What this means

Cookies and similar technologies on this website
TypeUsed?
Strictly necessary cookiesNone needed
Preference cookies and local storageNo
Session storageOne item: whether you closed the enquiry pop-up. Stays in your browser tab, cleared when you close it
Analytics cookies or scripts (e.g. Google Analytics)No
Advertising or social-media pixelsNo
Third-party embeds (maps, videos, chat, fonts from another server)No. Fonts are hosted on this site

Links to other services

If you tap a WhatsApp, Instagram or Google Maps link, you leave this website. Those services may set their own cookies under their own policies. We do not control them.

Your browser

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Doing so makes no difference to how this website works.

Consent on our contact form

The contact form asks you to tick a box before it will continue. The box records that you agree to us using the details you enter to answer your enquiry, as described in our Privacy Policy. The form does not send your details to us directly: it opens WhatsApp with your message ready, and nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. Nothing is stored in your browser.

If this changes

If we ever add analytics or any other non-essential cookie, we will update this page, list each cookie, and ask for your consent before it is set. You will be able to say no and to change your mind later.
