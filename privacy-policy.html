Privacy Policy

What We Collect

When you book a trip by phone, WhatsApp, or through this website's contact form, we collect the information needed to fulfil that booking — typically your name, phone number, pickup and drop locations, and travel dates. For corporate accounts, we also hold employee pickup-point lists shared by the client company for the purpose of running staff transport routes.

How We Use It

  • To confirm and coordinate your booking with a driver
  • To contact you about a trip, delay, or change
  • To generate invoices for corporate accounts
  • To improve routes and service based on booking patterns

What We Don't Do

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Information is shared only with the assigned driver as needed to complete the trip.

Data Retention

Booking records are retained for accounting and dispute-resolution purposes for a reasonable period, then deleted or anonymized.

Contact

For questions about this policy or to request your data be deleted, contact us at +91 93079 74659.
