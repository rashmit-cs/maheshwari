This policy explains how Maheshwari Tours and Travels ("we", "us") handles personal data when you use this website or contact us to book a trip. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the rules under it, and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and rules under it, as they apply to us.

What data we collect

We collect only what you choose to share so we can run the service you ask for. We do not ask for Aadhaar, PAN, card or bank details through this website.

How bookings work: we do not run an online booking system or keep a separate customer database. You contact us directly, usually on WhatsApp, and we agree the trip, the vehicle and the fare with you in that conversation. The details you share (such as your name, phone number and pickup details) therefore sit in our WhatsApp chats and call log. We give you a bill for the trip, and we may ask you afterwards to leave a review.

What this website does not do: it does not take payments or bookings online. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics, advertising or social-media tracking scripts, has no user accounts, and does not collect your location through your browser.

How we use it

  • To confirm, coordinate and carry out your booking with a driver
  • To contact you about a trip, delay, change or cancellation
  • To issue invoices and keep accounts, as tax and accounting law requires
  • To handle complaints, refunds and disputes
  • To meet legal obligations, for example requests from authorities

We use your data only for the purpose you gave it for. We do not use it for marketing unless you separately ask us to, and we do not make automated decisions about you.

Why we are allowed to use it

We process your data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you contact us or tick the box on the contact form, and for the "certain legitimate uses" the DPDP Act allows, such as performing a service you voluntarily requested and meeting legal obligations. You can withdraw consent at any time (see "Your rights"). Withdrawing does not affect what we did before you withdrew, and we may not be able to run a booking without the data it needs.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as follows:

  • The assigned driver: your name, phone number and trip details, so the trip can happen.
  • Your employer or school, for staff and school routes, about that route only.
  • Our web host, which handles the technical server logs described above, and the platforms named under "Third-party services" when you choose to use them.
  • Authorities, where the law or a court order requires it.

Third-party services and embeds

This website embeds no third-party content: there are no embedded maps, videos, social-media widgets, chat widgets, analytics or advertising scripts. Fonts are hosted on our own server, so loading a page does not send your IP address to a font provider.

Some links and buttons take you to services run by others. When you use them, that service handles your data under its own terms:

  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms): the "WhatsApp" buttons and the contact form open wa.me. Your message and phone number go to WhatsApp, which may process data outside India. Messages on WhatsApp are end-to-end encrypted by WhatsApp.
  • Google Maps: the "Office" link opens Google Maps in a new tab.
  • Instagram (Meta Platforms): the "Instagram" links open our Instagram profile in a new tab. Instagram handles your data under its own terms. We do not embed Instagram feeds or buttons on this website.
  • Phone calls go through your telecom provider.

Cookies

We set no cookies. See our Cookie Policy.

Retention and security

  • We do not keep a separate customer database. Your WhatsApp chat, call log entry and the bill for your trip stay with us only as long as we need them to handle the trip, any complaint or dispute, and any record that tax or accounting law requires us to keep. We have not set a fixed deletion period.
  • You can ask us to delete your WhatsApp chat and the details you shared, and we will do so unless the law requires us to keep them (for example a bill).
  • We share trip details only with the assigned driver and ask drivers to use them only for the trip. We take reasonable care to keep chats and records on our phones and devices from unauthorised access.
  • If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

Children's data

The website is not aimed at children. For school bus routes we receive the name, class, pickup address and guardian phone number of students, from the school or a parent or guardian. We process this only to run the route, and only with the verifiable consent of the parent or guardian (which the school or the guardian confirms to us). We do not track, profile or send advertising to children.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and who we shared it with;
  • ask us to correct, complete or update it;
  • ask us to erase it, unless the law requires us to keep it;
  • withdraw your consent at any time. This is as easy as giving it: call or message us;
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to;
  • get your grievance addressed.

To use any of these, call or message +91 93079 74659 or email Maheshwaritransport06@gmail.com. We may need to confirm it is you. We will reply as soon as we reasonably can, and in any case within the time the law allows.

Grievance and complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handled your data, write to our Grievance Officer (details above) first. If we do not resolve it, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India as provided under the DPDP Act.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when what we do with data changes and will change the date at the top. If we start using analytics, cookies or any third-party embed, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of India.
