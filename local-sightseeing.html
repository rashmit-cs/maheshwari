North Goa Circuit

Calangute Beach, Baga Beach, Anjuna Beach, Vagator Beach, Fort Aguada and Chapora Fort — typically covered in an 8-hour package.

South Goa Circuit

Basilica of Bom Jesus and the Old Goa churches, Shri Mangeshi Temple, Miramar Beach, the Panaji Latin Quarter, and spice plantation visits — typically covered in an 8 to 12-hour package.

How Hourly Rental Works

Pick a package — 4, 8 or 12 hours — and the vehicle plus driver stay dedicated to you for that window. Make unlimited stops for photos, shopping, food or a swim. Extra hours or kilometers beyond the package are billed at a pre-disclosed flat rate, agreed before you start.
