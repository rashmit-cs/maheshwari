    How the estimate works

    1. One-way and round trip: drag the slider (5 to 300 km). Distance × the per-km rate for your vehicle: 4-seater hatchback ₹16, 5-seater sedan ₹18, Ertiga-class ₹22, Innova Crysta / Hycross ₹24. A round trip doubles the distance. There is no minimum-kilometre charge. Trips under 25 km are priced as route fares, so we ask you to enquire.
    2. Airport, railway station and custom routes: listed routes have fixed fares, the same each way, with tolls and parking included. If your pickup and drop are not listed, choose "Custom route" and set the distance: distance × your vehicle's per-km rate. Tempo Traveller and bus airport fares are by seating.
    3. 8 hr / 80 km day package: a fixed fare for 8 hours and 80 km. Extra hours and kilometres are charged separately. Half-day and 12-hour packages are also quoted.
    4. Tempo Traveller and buses (9 to 40 seats): airport and 8 hr / 80 km fares are listed below. Trips by distance are quoted on request.

    Taxi or self-drive in Goa: what is worth comparing

    Self-drive rental

    • Rental charged per day.
    • You pay for fuel, parking and any tolls.
    • A refundable security deposit is held. It is money tied up, not money saved, and the rental agreement decides any deductions.
    • You drive and navigate yourself.

    Maheshwari taxi

    • Vehicle, driver and fuel are in the quoted fare.
    • Airport fares include tolls and parking.
    • No self-drive rental deposit.
    • A driver who knows Goa's roads, useful for families and groups.

    A taxi is not cheaper for every trip. A self-drive rental can cost less for a short trip with one or two people. The calculator shows the real comparison from your own inputs.
