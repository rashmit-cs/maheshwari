What We Run

Six services, one dispatch desk. Corporate contracts and one-off individual bookings, handled the same way — on time, on a fixed fare.

All Services Airport Transfer Outstation Trips Local Sightseeing Packages & Pricing

Corporate Staff Transport

Daily shift pickup and drop for plant and office staff, on fixed routes and fixed timings, with a dedicated point of contact. Currently running for teams at Siemens Energy, Persistent Systems, Digisol, Smartlink and UKB. Billed monthly against a fixed contract rate — no per-trip invoicing headaches.

Airport Transfers

Pickup and drop at Dabolim and Mopa airports, flight-tracked, with a driver waiting whether you land early or late. Fixed fares by route, no meter, no surge for late-night landings. See airport fares →

Outstation & Pilgrimage Trips

Inter-city runs and pilgrimage circuits out of Goa — Gokarna, Mumbai, Pune, Hampi, Belgaum and beyond — with a properly rested driver and one all-inclusive per-km fare. See outstation rates →

School Bus Service

Fixed-route school pickup and drop with attendance discipline, so parents and school offices always know where the bus is. Drivers vetted specifically for child-transport standards, not just a general taxi badge.

Event & Wedding Logistics

Guest transport for weddings, conferences and offsites — multiple vehicles coordinated from a single manifest, so nobody's left waiting on a curb.

Local Sightseeing & Custom Tours

North and South Goa sightseeing, built around your own timings rather than a fixed tour slot. Hourly rental packages with unlimited stops. See sightseeing packages →
