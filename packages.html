Corporate Contracts

Monthly Staff Transport

Priced per route based on distance, shift count and vehicle type, billed monthly against a fixed contract. This is how the corporate accounts on this site — Siemens Energy, Persistent Systems, Digisol, Smartlink and UKB — are set up. Get in touch with headcount and shift timings for a proposal.

School Bus Contracts

Priced per academic term based on route length and number of stops. Includes a dedicated driver assigned to the route for the full term, not rotated week to week.
