Cancellation Policy

Template — review before publishing

Individual Bookings

  • Cancellations made more than 2 hours before pickup: no charge
  • Cancellations made within 2 hours of pickup: a partial charge may apply to cover driver dispatch
  • No-shows (driver arrives, passenger doesn't): full fare may be charged

Outstation & Multi-Day Trips

Given advance vehicle and driver allocation, outstation bookings cancelled within 24 hours of the scheduled start may be subject to a cancellation charge. Contact the dispatch desk directly to discuss specific circumstances.

Corporate Contracts

Cancellation and route-change terms for corporate staff transport and school bus contracts are set out in the individual service agreement signed with each client.

How to Cancel

Call or WhatsApp +91 93079 74659 as early as possible — the sooner we know, the more flexibility we have.
