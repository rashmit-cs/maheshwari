About Maheshwari Tours and Travels

A Goa-based transport operator running corporate staff transport, taxi and bus services from a single dispatch desk.

What We Do

Maheshwari Tours and Travels runs corporate staff transport, airport transfers, outstation trips, school bus routes and event logistics across Goa. The business handles both long-term corporate contracts and one-off individual bookings through the same dispatch desk, so the standard of service doesn't change based on who's booking.

Corporate Clients

Current and past corporate transport clients include Siemens Energy, Persistent Systems, Digisol, Smartlink and UKB — running daily shift transport for plant and office staff on fixed routes and fixed timings.
