Refund Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Bookings are agreed with you directly on WhatsApp, and we accept cash and UPI payments depending on the arrangement we make with you. Many trips are paid at the end of the trip, so no refund is needed. This policy covers the cases where you have paid money in advance, paid for something you did not get, or been charged more than agreed. This website does not take online payments.

When you get a refund

Refund situations
SituationRefund
You paid an advance and we fail to provide the agreed serviceFull refund of the advance
We arrive at the agreed pickup location and you cancel or decline the serviceA cancellation or visit charge of ₹100 to ₹200 may be deducted from any advance, where applicable and where we have told you. The rest of the advance is returned
You were charged more than the fare we confirmedThe difference
Any other situation, for example a late cancellation, or a trip that differed from what we agreedAssessed according to the circumstances and the agreement made with you, after we hear both sides. We aim to be fair to both of us

What is not refunded

  • Trips completed as agreed
  • Tolls, permit taxes and parking already paid at actuals on your behalf
  • A change of mind after the trip has been completed

How to ask

Call or WhatsApp +91 93079 74659 with your name, the date of the trip, how you paid and what went wrong. Please tell us as soon as you can after the trip, so we can check with the driver while it is fresh. We will reply as soon as we can.

How you are paid

Where a refund is due, we return it in cash or by UPI, whichever suits you. We will tell you when we approve the refund how soon you can expect it. We may ask for your UPI ID only to send the refund.

Corporate and school contracts

Credits and adjustments follow the signed service agreement.

If you disagree with our decision

Write to our Grievance Officer (see the Contact page). You can also use the National Consumer Helpline (1915, consumerhelpline.gov.in (opens in a new tab)) or approach the consumer commission with jurisdiction under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
